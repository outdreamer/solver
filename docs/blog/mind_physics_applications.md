# Mind Physics Applications


The following mind-physics applications are useful to identify/apply:

1. 'mentally implementing intents' with a 'level of specificity' that matches the 'level of consciousness' (meaning that 'comprehending the complete specific implementation of the intent, such as comprehending its inputs/requirements and outputs/effects, comprehending as in being conscious of it' is required to 'implement an intent mentally')
	- 'mentally implementing intents' is more trivial/optimized when:
		- the intent is 'trivial to understand/comprehend', and the intent is optimal/relevant in some way so it's useful such as 'incentivized/required' to implement
		- the implementer is extremely practiced at 'optimization' functions and can 'trivialize most intents (identify an efficient way to implement an intent)'
		- there is a trivial way to implement the intent (like 'creating a simulation/hologram/description/graph of an intent, rather than implementing the actual intent as its usually defined')
		- the implementer has practiced/experienced it before in detail and has a detailed memory of it
		- the understanding is 'specific enough to implement the intent accurately/realistically'
		- the implementer is 'conscious of reality and its complexity and other variables'
		- the implementer has a 'powerful imagination'
		- the 'will to implement the intent' is powerful
		- the connection between the implementer/implemented structures is multi-directional (like 'two-way')
		- there are no 'opposing intents' being implemented by a more powerful mind or physics process and it doesnt 'contradict reality'
		- the implementer 'frequently uses the functions required to implement it'
		- the implementer has a 'relatively high degree of consciousness/understanding', enough to handle the 'complexity and errors of implementing the intent'
	- 'mentally implementing intents' to a degree of reality that is 'sufficient to identify the errors/limits/relevance of the intent' is often more useful than completely 'mentally (or physically) implementing the intent'
	- 'mentally implementing intents' in an optimized way involves, for example, 'identifying the most useful intents to implement (like future useful intents that enable many other useful intents)'

2. 'inventing/implementing/applying a program to compute a unit/error/structure of reality' is another application of mind-physics interactions that is useful to identify (like 'computing a permanent/absolute limit of reality'), which 'determines reality by identifying/computing reality', which has an impact on 'remaining relevant variation' (like 'computing too high a ratio of reality would over-determine it and leave insufficient relevant variation for reality to continue existing')
	- similarly, 'computing a way for reality to expand (as in, for the relevant variation of reality to increase)' is a related mind-physics interaction
