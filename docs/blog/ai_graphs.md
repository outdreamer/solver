# AI Graphs


## Graph Types

Useful types of graphs of AI structures include:
- proof graphs (for AI-related proofs, like 'proving that all possible error types are indirectly/distantly accessible or blocked by barriers on every relevant graph')
- intent graphs (for AI intents like 'coordinate different models' and 'distill models into understanding-based graphs')
- other interface structure graphs (like 'causal graphs of parameter/priority causality of error structures' and 'graphs of generative variable causality of emergent functionality' and 'graphs of AI errors that identify inferred/derived/missing errors')
- AI structure interaction graphs (like 'graphs of emergent interaction types of AI agents like "shared memory"' and 'graphs of interactions of AI models like specialization/organization/voting')
- AI optimization graphs (like 'graphs of AI parameters/models and their optimal interactions' and 'graphs of optimizations of AI models')


## Graph Usages

These graphs are useful beyond the usefulness of 'general relevant graphs (like concept optimization graphs)' bc AI is a poorly defined interface that would benefit from interface analysis, including identifying relevant graphs.

Useful usages of these graphs include:
- identifying 'similarities across AI parameters/models' to identify 'similarities in error types/causes'
- identifying absolute qualities of AI models (like an 'AI model proven not to cause an error type, for these intents and cases') to identify AI models that can be applied as constants (like 'AI models that can be combined to reliably generate another AI model of a specific type')
