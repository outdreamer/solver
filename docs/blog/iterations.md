# Iterations

What structures are useful, even when iterated in a simple way:
- the definition of relevance/importance/optimality/meaning/usefulness hasn't stopped being useful yet, despite applying it thousands of times, since it isn't yet fully specified by its applications/interactions
- specific graphs like relevant graphs like the similarity index graph haven't stopped being useful yet, despite their interactions being specified/described many times
- graphs haven't stopped being useful yet, despite identifying hundreds of relevant graphs
- algorithms are still useful
- core intents like filter/optimize/organize/integrate/generate are still useful

Why are these structures so useful, such as being consistently useful:
- one reason they're useful is that they are interface structures, but not all interface structures are equally useful, some are more adjacently and consistently/generally useful than other structures
- they are maximally different structures which contain high degrees of variation, allowing for approximately infinite complexity, ensuring their constant relevance
- they relate to relevance, which is a requirement of being relevant
- they occupy a unique position of relevance, such as being a common useful structure like a 'common input to useful structures' or a similarly relevant position

What structures are not useful when simply iterated:
- simple/basic/default/otherwise irrelevant structures/connections as in a 'graph of connections indicated by simple/structural similarity' are not always useful when iterated because relevant similarity is more useful by comparison, and simple connections require more iteration to be relevant
- iterations themselves can't always be applied iteratively to generate relevance

What does this mean for algorithms/graphs:
- this means 'iteration' has various positions where it's useful, like when:
	- iterating abstraction level to generate a 'graph of graphs'
	- iterating interface analysis to some relevant degree to identify interface structure interactions to optimize
	- iterating as in copying a network so it can be tested/varied or can generate functionality like 'self-evaluation' or 'memory'
- but in general, iteration is not relevant on its own, without:
	- other relevant structures integrated
	- a high degree of iteration
