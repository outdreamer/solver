# After the optimal graph of graphs


## Graph intents

What is after the optimal graph of graphs?

1. Generating new problems as an outer layer of the graph of graphs, and generating new relevant graphs to solve new problems, for which applying a circle of relevant interface variables is useful, to identify for example 'volatility errors' and 'volatility-complexity errors' and so on, at every iteration of the graph of graphs

2. Generating optimizations of the graph to implement 'new emergent optimal system interaction rules' and 'new optimization metrics/intents' identified by the optimal graph of graphs, optimizations like 'applying multi-functionality' for example to 'limits' of the graph, like by making 'limits of the graph' also 'filters of errors to prevent errors from integrating with the graph'.

3. Increasing relevant variation like by 'integrating new relevant graphs in new ways with new structures'

4. Generating higher-dimensional graphs than the graph of graphs and identifying higher-dimensional graphs that aren't simplified by the graph of graphs

5. Applying iterations of the optimal graph of graphs as a 'component' structure and as other structures to identify the errors of the optimal graph of graphs


## Graph intent sequences/graphs

Given a graph intent sequence like 'generate/organize/optimize/integrate a graph', what comes after identifying/generating a graph is optimizing it and fitting it into a graph of relevant graphs. 

1. generate alternate optimal graphs of graphs, like by applying interface analysis to the optimal graph of graphs until enough differences are identified to identify an alternate optimal graph of graphs

2. generate a graph of graphs of graphs

3. integrate the optimal graph of graphs with other relevant graphs (apply/implement/otherwise integrate the optimal graph of graphs to other relevant graphs)

4. identifying optimal ways to generate/integrate/organize/optimize/apply the optimal graph of graphs, to create a network of all possible optimal ways to generate/integrate/organize/optimize/apply the optimal graph of graphs

5. identifying interface graphs like 'limit/error/optimization/query graphs' of the optimal graph of graphs to identify the relevance of the optimal graph of graphs

6. generate graphs of variants of intent graphs to guide the generation of other graphs implementing these intent graphs
