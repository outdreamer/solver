# Errors of interface structures

There are many possible errors in applying interface structures.


1. "The system of optimal interactions between interface variables is just a network of concept nodes"

This is false because the system of optimal interactions between interface variables also includes nodes like combinations/structures of interface variables, systems of interface variables, relevant graphs of interface variables, and other integrated/iterated structures that are required to reflect a realistic level of complexity and other metrics in real systems.

The same applies to "the concept definition network is just a network of concept nodes" which is false because the concept definition network includes 'alternate definition routes of a concept' and 'variables like specificity/implementation/structures of concepts'.

The same applies to "the graph of relevant graphs is just a network of graphs as nodes" which is false because there are many variants of this graph with graph variables like 'connection types', and the 'graph of relevant graphs' also reflects optimal interactions between relevant graphs, optimal applications of relevant graphs, optimal implementations of relevant graphs, and other structures of relevant graphs.


2. "Iterating interface variables is all you need"

Just like 'complexity of complexity of complexity' is not useful by default, without other structures like 'other iterations of interface variables' and 'optimal interactions of iterations of interface variables', so other iterations of interface variables aren't relevant/useful without other structures applied with them. 

This is similar to how generating structures isnt useful without also filtering/evaluating/understanding structures.


3. "Keep applying graphs of graphs until all structures are identified"

'Identifying structures' isnt the only intent to optimize for - where other intents include 'optimizing structures', 'applying structures', 'integrating structures', etc. The interactions of graphs are more complicated than just 'identifying the position of a graph on another graph', which is useful for 'compressing a graph to a point to identify similar graphs', but there are other graph intents and graph interactions that dont require a 'graph compressed to a point' but rather require the 'graph in its full implementation to enable queries on the graph' or the 'generative variables of the graph' or other structures of the graph.

Also, other structures than 'graphs of graphs' can be useful for 'identifying similar graphs' or 'identifying structures in general', like 'queries of graphs', 'sequences of graphs', 'queries of sequences of graphs', 'queries of sequences of graph intents', 'interface graphs of a graph', 'alternates of a graph', the 'integrated/optimized form of a graph', etc.

Regarding point 1, a graph of graphs isnt useful without other structures, like 'applications/optimizations/integrations/alternates of the graph of graphs' and 'structures to evaluate/filter the graph of graphs'.
