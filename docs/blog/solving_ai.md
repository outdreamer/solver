# Solving AI

It's useful to compare requirements to solving problems in different problem spaces.


## Solving math

'Solving math' has requirements like:
- identifying the optimal graph of math structure interactions
- trivially/optimally solving for all possible relevant sets of optimization metrics/intents in every specific math problem space


## Solving graphs

'Solving graphs' has requirements like:
- identifying the optimal graph of relevant graphs, the optimal graph space to apply that graph in, identifying optimal graph intents to solve, and applying the optimal graph to solve those intents
- identifying optimal graph completion/generation/optimization/reduction/connection functions


## Solving optimization

'Solving optimization' has requirements like:
- identifying the optimal graph of the definition of optimality/relevance (and related graph variants, like 'the graph of causality/requirements/variants of optimality/relevance') and the optimal interactions/applications of these graphs
- solving 'trivialization of problem-solving' (like 'identifying comparison graphs that make a problem trivial to solve') or solving 'limits' or solving alternate intents that are related to solving 'optimization'


## Solving problem-solving

Solving problem-solving has requirements like:
- identifying/defining all possible relevant problem types/structures (like 'bottleneck problems (which once solved, will enable optimization cascades)')
- identifying comparable optimizations to 'optimizations of problem structures'
- identifying all possible relevant ways to generate higher solution/problem ratios


## Solving AI

'Solving AI' has requirements like:
- identifying a function that can trivially/optimally solve complex problems like 'identifying relevant structures' and 'connecting relevant graphs' and 'solving math' and 'identifying optimal intents to solve'
- applying this function to solve the optimal intents
- identifying a function that can optimize any structure for any structure/set of optimization metrics/intents


## Problem Space interactions

Solving these problem spaces (like graphs or problems) have similar requirements to be considered 'solved/completed/covered/described/defined/understood/invalidated/structured/isolated/simplified', and can enable/invalidate/overlap with each other.

'Identifying the optimal intents to solve across these problem spaces' is useful to 'filter computation', like: 
- identify the most relevant graphs of optimality learnable/usable by the most relevant AI models to optimize understanding of graph spaces/intents
- identify the most useful applications of the most optimal identified relevant graphs of math and identify optimized paths to those useful application implementations
