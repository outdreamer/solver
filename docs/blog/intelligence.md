# Intelligence

Why is it important to be intelligent?

Functions/variables of intelligence:
- enables problem-solving (it can solve any problem, like stupidity/vices/addiction)
- can identify/generate/change/determine/cause all the other virtues/solutions/optimizations (virtues like 'independence', or optimizations like 'functions to avoid suboptimalities' like 'functions to avoid crimes')
- can identify/generate/change/determine/cause any variable (like time or luck or optimality)
- can sustain/increase/distribute intelligence
- intelligence (as in 'relevant thinking') is the right way to earn power
- intelligence is a solution, and is therefore related to other solutions
- the meaning of intelligence is 'functionality/power/potential', and it 'enables functionality/power/potential'
- intelligence is the only variable that is relevant, bc of its connection to the other variables (like its potential to determine/generate the other variables)
- reality is complicated, and intelligence is required to solve it

Functions/variables of stupidity:
- is easily changed into other vices/problems (stupidity is a problem, and is therefore related to other problems)
- causes problems, rather than solving problems
- can only see one false solution (as in 'crime, like copying/scams/lies')
- can only trivially identify/change/copy its inputs (which is worthless)
- cannot identify/generate any virtues/optimizations (and therefore cannot see/solve reality)
- stupidity is irrelevant
