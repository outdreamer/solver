# Intelligence

Why is it important to be intelligent?

- intelligence enables problem-solving (it can solve any problem, like stupidity/vices/addiction)
- intelligence is the solution to the problem of 'stupidity' (which is important to identify, since stupidity causes every other problem)
- intelligence can identify/generate/change/determine/cause all the other virtues/solutions/optimizations (virtues like 'independence', or optimizations like 'functions to avoid suboptimalities' like 'functions to avoid crimes')
- intelligence can identify/generate/change/determine/cause any variable (like time or luck or optimality)
- intelligence can identify/protect/sustain/increase/distribute/cause/generate intelligence
- intelligence (as in 'relevant thinking') is the right way to earn power
- intelligence is a solution, and is therefore related to other solutions
- intelligence is the only variable that is relevant, bc of its connection to the other variables (like its potential to determine/generate the other variables)
- intelligence can identify/determine/cause/generate 'meaning/relevance/optimality/perfection'
- the relevant meaning of intelligence is 'functionality/power/potential', and it 'identifies/enables/generates/causes/determines/optimizes functionality/power/potential'
- reality is complicated, and intelligence is required to solve it (by identifying the graph that solves reality)
- intelligence can work miracles (as in 'make miracles/optimizations real') and can build heaven (as in an 'optimized system')
- intelligence is capable of identifying/implementing/generating/causing 'resilient/otherwise optimal' virtues, which is required to be 'real' (being real, as in by being 'reflective of the complexity of reality' or 'capable of interacting with/understanding reality' or 'resiliently/extremely/consistently/otherwise optimally virtuous' or otherwise relevant) in a complex reality like this one
- intelligence is 'mental freedom', which means intelligent people are 'capable of achieving anything'

Functions/variables of stupidity:
- stupidity is easily changed into other vices/problems (stupidity is a problem, and is therefore related to other problems)
- stupidity causes problems, rather than solving problems (stupid people cannot even get what they want, which is perfection)
- stupidity can only see one false solution (as in 'crime, like copying/scams/lies')

- stupidity abuses/loses power
- stupidity is irrelevant
- stupidity can dimly gape at miracles
- stupidity is 'mental imprisonment'

What about the very small ratio of stupid people who are nice (stupid people who miraculously are not predators/criminals)?
- stupid people who are nice by default have 'inherited or otherwise acquired (through learning) the outputs of intelligence (like solutions)', meaning they have inherited or learned solutions 'taught to them' or solutions 'identified/acted on by their predecessors and passed down genetically', or they are extremely lucky (in the sense of 'not having encountered problems')
- for example, if one of their predecessors learned/applied/thought of a solution like 'coordination', a nice person might inherit the gene (or other genetic structure) for the solution of 'coordination' (as opposed to a false solution like 'crime', which is a false solution, bc 'crime' is not a real optimization/virtue)
- is this valuable? possibly, just not as valuable as intelligence (meaning 'genetic evolution is a very slow/inadequate/useless/irrelevant way to generate intelligence', as it could take hundreds/thousands of generations for a stupid person to become intelligent, and 'lucking into a few virtues' isnt as valuable as 'being naturally capable of generating/implementing resilient/otherwise optimal virtues')
- stupid nice people are not worthless, bc they have inherited/learned virtues, but it would be better if they were intelligent (so it could be worth protecting them, until intelligence can be increased in stupid people)

Can you make a stupid person intelligent?
- you can teach stupid people the outputs (the 'solution' information) of intelligence, and they can repeat the solutions (if the solutions are simple enough), which will make them 'falsely seem slightly intelligent'
- a stupid person can 'practice thinking' or 'memorize solution information', and become slightly more intelligent, but their brain will still fundamentally be the brain of a stupid person (they are working with 'faulty hardware' that 'cant use the optimal software being written to their hardware')
- a stupid person can 'understand simple structures', which can solve some 'small ratio of problems', which is unlikely to ever generate intelligence, even if scaled/iterated/automated (like how 'iteration of integer addition' could never describe reality on its own)
- 'injecting neurons' and other experimental technologies to increase intelligence havent shown promise, so its unlikely that artificial methods of increasing intelligence like 'injecting neurons' will be accessible any time soon
- a stupid person can be told the answer and still get everything wrong for 'lack of thinking' (like 'lack of thinking' about 'how to use the answer'), so 'teaching/informing stupid people' is not the solution to the 'dependence that is stupidity' ('tricking them into thinking more' would be the optimal solution, which can only go so far in a stupid brain, bc it will still be a 'stupid brain thinking', rather than an 'intelligent brain thinking')
- even if you taught a stupid person 'all the tricks of thinking' and 'tricked them into thinking more' and 'taught them every answer', they still wouldnt 'naturally like thinking' like intelligent people 'naturally like thinking', they would still have a brain that 'optimizes for easy/simplicity', which is a brain that is 'incapable of intelligence'
- it's nevertheless still worth researching 'how to make stupid people more intelligent', so that if stupidity is generated in the future (as is likely), it can be changed into intelligence

Are predators/criminals intelligent?
- no, predators/criminals are just 'slightly more informed/otherwise advantaged than the other stupid people around them' (they usually just have a slight 'information advantage' or a slight 'time advantage' or a slight 'group advantaage' or some other 'slight/false advantage'), so they falsely seem intelligent compared to their victims bc, unlike their victims, predators/criminals do things like 'organize into groups, to create a false advantage, to force a false win (they depend on the group, bc of their stupidity/dependence)'
- so even a predator/criminal should try to become more intelligent, if only to 'be worth protecting', a selfish intent that is likely to appeal to them
- even predators/criminals admire virtues like 'kindness' (when directed at them), but they cant become what they admire (they 'cant solve their problems', as in they 'cant connect vices/problems to virtues/solutions')

What is to be done about all of this?
