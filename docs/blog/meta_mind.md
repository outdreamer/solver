# Meta-mind

It's useful to describe a meta-mind, given the usefulness of defining mind types.


## Definition

What is a meta-mind?
- a mind that is composed of other minds


## Variables

- a meta-mind can exist under a 'unique mind-type interaction' limit, where only one conscious mind and one subconscious mind are 'plugged in' at any given time, as opposed to 'multiple conscious minds simultaneously interacting with multiple subconscious minds', but this is variable across meta-minds
- 'which minds compose the meta-mind' is another variable of a meta-mind


## Defining abilities

The defining abilities identifying a meta-mind are:
- it can simulate/identify/generate/apply/understand/predict/analyze any other mind
- it can simulate interactions of different mind types/different minds
- it can change its own component minds/interactions between components
- it generally has a central mind that controls the other minds for efficiency


## Component minds

Each component mind can:
- take the form of a graph (a graph can connect problems/solutions, so it can solve problems, so it's a new way of thinking, so a graph can be applied as a mind)
- apply specific interface structures (a mind can apply a 'perspective/set of priorities/core functions/common intents')


## Other types of mind

Other types of mind include:
- a hive mind (one mind controls a group of people/agents)
- a genius mind (a mind with a scaled/otherwise optimal intelligence type)
- a relevance mind (a mind with a specific intelligence type, like relevance/optimization intelligence, like how a 'relevance mind' can take the form of a 'brain of relevant graphs as nodes')


## Differences from a group of people/agents

- a meta-mind is not just a 'group of people/AI agents', bc one person can model many minds, and each mind can be 'inhabited/understood/applied' in a way that a different person cant
- a person can 'live in one of their component minds', and 'occasionally visit other component minds', and 'allow one component mind to take control of the other minds', which is different from how people/agents interact
- a meta-mind is not just a 'set of mental models' either, similar to how it's not just a 'set of priorities/functions'


## Meta-mind, on the scale of intelligence

Where does a meta-mind fall on the scale of intelligence?

- a meta-mind has high potential/power, but it's not required to implement/apply that potential, so it can overlap with a normal smart person, all the way up to a super-genius
- a meta-mind can have other intelligent minds as its component minds (a meta-mind can have a relevance mind as one of its component minds), so there is overlap between mind types in that way as well
