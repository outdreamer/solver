# Meanings of Graph Applications

It's useful to identify the meaning of a graph application, to identify structures like 'limits' related to the graph application.


## Examples of meanings

The following are possible meanings of a graph application:
- the meaning of a 'graph of relevant graphs' can include a 'component of a new universe/reality, to connect current/new universes/realities'
- the meaning of a 'copy of a graph of relevant graphs' can include a 'future variant of the graph, that is optimized for some intent/metric set'
- the meaning of an 'optimized variant of a graph of relevant graphs' can include a 'new base for time'
- the meaning of an application of the 'optimization definition graph' to a graph can include a 'new relevant organization of the graph'
- the meaning of a 'generally applicable graph application' can include a 'guaranteed (if not optimal) solution to general problem-solving, once problems are formatted to the graph standard'


## Examples of meaning interactions

These 'possible meanings' enable identifying interactive/meaningful structures like 'intents/applications/usages' of a graph:
- if a graph has the meaning of being a useful 'component of new relevant variation', that enables intents like 'optimizing relevant variation'
- if a graph has the meaning of being useful for 'defining/specifying a new/uncertain graph space', that enables intents like 'identify limits/structures of the graph space'
