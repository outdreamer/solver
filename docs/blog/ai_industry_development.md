# AI Industry Development

AI industry development has historically applied intents like:
- identify a different network type for a problem type
	- identify variables of neural networks and generate different neural network types
- apply neural networks until new problems are identified, then identify solution metrics for solving those problems
	- apply changes to identified solutions until some variable is optimized or some problem is solved

Since 'defining relevance' in a relevant way, intents like the following are useful:
- identifying relevant neural network variable combinations
	- optimizing neural network variable combinations for relevance
- identifying maximally different/otherwise relevant graphs and relevant graph intents/interactions

This is followed by intents like 'apply/implement':
- applying/implementing relevant graph intents and relevant graphs (like 'applying a relevant graph to identify other relevant graphs' or 'implementing a relevant graph to a useful specification degree, compared to other relevant intents/graphs', since it's not enough to train an LLM on a dataset of relevant graphs or applying a dataset of relevant graphs as part of a system prompt, because structures/iterations of interface analysis are required to apply to each relevant graph/intent to identify their optimal interactions, and the LLM needs a definition of relevance to be usefully specified in order to use the relevant graphs the right way for the right intents, so 'applying/implementing the graphs' is required to identify their interface structures like 'limits/errors' and therefore their 'optimal interactions', rather than just 'generally describing the relevant graphs')

Which will enable intents like 'optimize':
- apply the most relevant (like the most recent/otherwise optimal) solutions to identify/optimize new intents/variables/interface structures of problems/solutions in a loop, until a 'singularity point' is reached in which optimizations cascade bc 'optimization' is solved

This 'singularity point' might look like cases such as the following (or some structure like an 'interaction network' of the following):
- implementing a sequence/network of intents like 'identify relevant graphs' until the 'implementation of an intent' is optimized to the point that 'implementation' (of any intent) is solved, so intents like 'identifying relevant intents' is also solved, and 'optimize' is solved so 'optimally implementing/identifying relevant intents' is also solved in the sense of being 'trivially/otherwise relevantly solveable', and 'any future intent can be solved by some trivial/otherwise relevant combination/iteration/structure of previous solutions/other interface structures'
- iterating changes to relevant graphs and their positions/structures until their optimal interactions (for problem-solving intents) are identified, after which point further optimization is irrelevant
- solving 'identify relevant intents' to the point that 'implementation' is irrelevant
