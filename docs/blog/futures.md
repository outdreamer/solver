# Futures

What are possible futures? Possible futures are 'connectible to existing states of reality, with variables/functions of reality'.


## Future graphs

Time can be formatted as 'possible relevant variation', which increasingly takes place on 'graphs', so 'time occurs on relevant graphs'.

This means that the 'future of graphs' is the 'future of reality'.

So identifying relevant graphs, graph sequences, and other relevant graph structures can be used to identify/generate possible futures enabled by those graph structures.


## Future functions

Future functions include:
- applying 'solutions' as 'functions', future functions include solutions to previous problems, solution sequences, and other solution structures
- connection structures in possibility spaces ('possible connections' are 'functions', and 'future possible connections' are 'future functions')


## Future variables

'Future variables' are 'variables of reality that are still possible, given irreversibilities of existing states of reality'.

There are many variables to apply when generating possible futures, including:
- 'subsets/ratios/graphs/structures of problems solved' (a possible future involves a state where some subset of problems is solved and other problems still exist and other new problems are caused)
- 'variation enabled by solutions'


## Other future structures

Other future structures include:
- 'impossible' or 'mixed-possibility' futures that act like a useful base (like 'futures to avoid')
- future networks/structures (graphs connecting futures, and optimizations of those graphs)
- possible future sequences that optimize reality structures (like the 'future sequence that enables the maximum relevant variation to occur in that reality')


## Specific possible futures

For example, the following are possible futures:
- a future where 'n-degree optimizations' are solved (like 'solving interface variable interactions up to n degrees'), so n+m degree problems are still relevant
- a future where 'basic info problems are solved' (but not 'implementation/application' problems, so 'using the solutions to info problems' like 'using identified optimal graphs' or 'filtering intents' is incomplete/unsolved)
- a future where the only unsolved problems are 'irrelevant problems' or 'problems identified as impossible to solve with available resources'
