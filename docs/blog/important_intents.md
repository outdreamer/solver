# Important intents

Beyond identifying 'what work remains to be done' to 'automate problem-solving' and 'apply the tool to solve relevant problems', other intents are especially important.


Important intents include:
- maintaining a 'level/degree/structure of relevant variation' that exceeds 'irrelevant variation (like chaos)'
- identifying the 'problem/solution ratio to aim for' that will maintain the required 'level of relevant variation'
- identifying the 'relevant/important problems/solutions' to include in that ratio calculation, and the relevant graph to identify/optimize relevant problem/solution interactions (like 'problems that could invalidate a high ratio of solutions' and an 'optimal problem/solution graph like a graph with sufficiently distributed solutions to prevent problems from taking over the graph')
- identifying 'specific problem/solution structures to maintain' like 'specific problems to solve (or be kept unsolved)' is useful to 'maintain a relevant level of relevant variation' 
- maintaining a 'degree/direction/network/structure of new changes' that is 'provably safe/otherwise relevant' like it 'can be trivially managed by existing/adjacent solutions'
- identifying problem/solution structures (like 'specific problems to solve that generate many optimal solutions') that 'increase the difference between relevant/irrelevant variation, or increase relevant variation'
- identifying the 'problems to solve' which will enable/trivialize/optimize these intents


These intents are important bc:
- 'keeping solutions more numerous/distributed/relevant/prioritized/certain than problems' is useful to 'stay ahead of new problems' to guarantee 'always being able to solve new problems'
- these intents are general, not well-specified/defined, not automated/solved yet, and are evaluatable/testable enough for a person to evaluate them in some cases, so they are still useful for a person to think about


'Identifying other "interface structure interactions" (other than problem/solution or relevance/irrelevance interactions) that are important to maintain/aim for/generate/optimize' is the general intent to complete.
