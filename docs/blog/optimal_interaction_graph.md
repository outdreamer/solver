# Optimal system interactions of relevant graphs

What does it mean to 'generate a graph of optimal system interactions of relevant graphs'?

An 'optimal system of interaction rules' involves 'optimizing the interactions of rules' like 'what optimal combinations/sequences/conditions to apply rules in'.

'Relevant graphs' (graphs that are unique/optimal/useful/independent/new/complex/high variation, relevant to optimization metrics and optimal intents, and relevant to other graphs) are defined here:
https://github.com/outdreamer/solver/blob/master/docs/blog/relevant_graphs.md

A system graph implements 'interactions between system structures' like 'components' (components interact, therefore generating a system, as opposed to independent structures, which aren't part of the system, because they don't interact with the system), indicating the context/interactivity/relevance of system structures.

## Definition of the optimal system interaction graph

An 'optimal system of interaction rules' is useful to identify as a core/base graph to start from, to connect rules like 'applying these graph intents in this optimal sequence'.

Once this system graph of optimal interaction rules is identified, it can be applied to connect relevant graphs.

Examples of optimal interactions of relevant graphs (connections on the system of interaction rules of relevant graphs):
- 'apply these graphs in this sequence to optimize for low-volatility, high-relevance, complex variation'
- 'connect these opposite relevant graphs with a set of connecting/interim/average graphs or a graph of graphs to connect maximal differences'
- 'apply these integrations of graphs to generate these more optimal graphs'
- 'apply the optimal graph generation/optimization/reduction intent sequence to implement graphs optimally'

The optimal system of interactions of relevant graphs would also implement/contain/organize structures like:
- a 'causal sequence of graphs that optimally generates more relevant graphs from less relevant graphs'
- 'nodes of the optimal system of interaction rules of relevant graphs' would be relevant graphs or structures/combinations/sets/sequences of relevant graphs, where a variant of this would include algorithms/queries/interface structures as nodes/connections of the optimal system of interaction rules
- a 'useful abstraction layer' to start applying changes to, to generate relevant graphs
- a set of 'useful spectrums of relevant graphs' to increase the level of optimization metrics implemented by a graph sequence
- 'optimal causal interactions' would be applied to generate 'a system of optimal causal graphs/sequences' as a subset of the optimal system of interactions of relevant graphs, either as graph components, core/base/component graphs, graph layers, or similar alternate structures, and similarly 'optimal optimization interactions' would be applied to generate a 'system of optimal optimization graphs/sequences', and so on for other subsets of optimal interactions like 'optimal implementation/organization/integration/generation interactions'

## How to generate the system of optimal interactions of relevant graphs

Examples of starting points to generate the optimal system of interaction rules of relevant graphs:
- 'apply relevant graphs (like the "circle of extremes of spectrums from the intersecting spectrum graph") of relevant graphs (to identify extremes of spectrums of relevant graphs)'
- 'apply graphs of relevant graphs as limits of the solution space of possible relevant graph interactions'
- 'apply another level of variation like organization/optimization/integration to relevant graphs to generate a set of graphs to apply as limits of optimizations of the core graphs'
- 'apply interface graphs to relevant graphs until relevant graphs are trivially/otherwise usefully connected, then optimize this set of connections'
- 'apply intent graphs to generate associated relevant graphs implementing points on those intent graphs, to connect relevant graphs by intents'
- 'apply all possible relevant connection types between relevant graphs and filter optimal connections'
- 'integrate the maximum relevant graphs in a graph, and iterate until a set of graphs integrating relevant graphs is identified, then connect those graphs integrating relevant graphs'
- 'apply the base optimal system of interaction rules as a starting graph and fit relevant graphs to this base system graph until changes/optimizations/structures are required'
- 'describe the optimal system of interaction rules of relevant graphs until its specified enough to be trivially/uniquely implementable'

Once the system of optimal interactions of relevant graphs is identified, queries like the following can be run:
- identify relevant graphs to solve a graph optimization intent
- identify optimizations/errors/interface structures that connect these graphs
- identify maximal differences possible on the system of optimal interactions of relevant graphs

The optimal system of interactions of relevant graphs would implement the intents:
- 'optimize relevant graphs', 'organize relevant graphs', 'identify relevant graphs', 'connect relevant graphs', etc
- 'optimize relevant graphs for graph intents', 'optimize relevant graphs for relevant graph integrations', etc
- 'implement useful structures/degrees of interface analysis'

## Why generate the system of optimal interactions of relevant graphs

The point of the system of optimal interactions of relevant graphs is to identify how relevant graphs would optimally interact, as in how relevant graphs would be applied/organized/integrated/optimized/connected/implemented. The core graph of optimal system interactions that applies to systems in general may need to be optimized for application to relevant graphs - the interactions that optimize a 'system of functions' may not apply equivalently to optimize a 'system of graphs', so the core graph of optimal system interactions is useful to apply as a starting graph but is unlikely to cover every optimal interaction of relevant graphs on its own, as in it may require specification for relevant graphs like by optimizing for graph intents.

Why would you optimize the system of interaction rules of relevant graphs? To 'maximize the optimal variation represented in a system', which allows the maximum problems to be solved in that system (system/graph/network).

Implementing this graph would allow 'skipping a lot of experimentation with interface analysis', instead applying identified optimals, like the 'maximum relevant variation structures already identified' (relevant graphs) as bases/components of the optimal system interaction graph, since it's already defined that these graphs are relevant/optimal and can be applied as a base of a workflow to implement interface analysis to a relevant degree/structure.
