## Generating relevant graphs

Graphs are relevant if they:
- implement/optimize a relevant intent like 'identify relevant graphs' or 'connect relevant graphs/structures' or 'organize relevant graphs' or 'implement optimal system interaction rules'
- are evaluatable/interactive/new/independent/implement some other combination/structure of components of relevance/meaning/usefulness
- apply cross-interface connections/structures (for relevance/interactivity) rather than applying one interface or one interface structure

This definition of relevance can be applied to generate relevant graphs, and relevant graphs can also be applied to generate relevant graphs.
