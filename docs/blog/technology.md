# Technology

Technology should be evaluated by its interface structures.

## Meaning

- what worlds does the technology end? ('relevance/intent/usefulness/optimality/importance/meaning/innovation' technology ends the worlds of 'irrelevance, pointlessness, uselessness, suboptimality, triviality, meaninglessness, and constants' for example)
- how does it interact with other technologies ('relevance/optimization/innovation' technology ends the requirement for many other technologies/intents like 'competition')
- how does it interact with other interface variables (what governments, institutions, religions, systems, economies, futures, interactions does it optimize/invalidate/organize/integrate/filter/change?)

## Potential

- what could the technology be easily/probably/otherwise relevantly used for?
- what could the technology easily/probably/otherwise relevantly change/filter/apply/organize/become?
- what can't the technology implement/organize/optimize?

## Requirement

- what are the technology's requirements/possibilities?
- what does the technology require/guarantee? What impossibilities/possibilities/probabilities does it change?

## Structure

- what are relevant graphs of the technology?
- what is the technology's position/structure on the graph of requirements/possibilities and other relevant graphs?
- what graphs does the technology change/generate/filter/apply?

## Intent

- what is the technology intended for?
- does it achieve that intent optimally?
- what else does it implement trivially/otherwise relevantly?

## Iteration

- what is likely to occur if the technology is applied iteratively?
- what are relevant iterations of the technology?

## Generality

- how generally can this technology be optimally applied?
- what level of specificity is optimal for this technology?

## Volatility

- what changes create a high degree of change in the technology?
- can the technology become deterministic?

## Optimality

- is the technology likely to be implemented optimally?
- what is the optimal implementation of the technology?
