# AI and Group Dynamics


## Solving resource distribution

AI/software enables groups to optimize their resource distribution, so that a group can solve the problem of 'distributing resources fairly' with algorithms or AI models.


## Solving group incentives in government

This also solves the problem of 'the government being a group' which means the 'governments responds to group incentives such as "negating the interests of other groups"'.

By building their own AI models/algorithms/inventions, each group can avoid the 'incentive problems of a group (like the government) which is in charge of solving problems for other groups'.


## Solving inter-group conflict

This introduces new problems, like 'how to manage the conflicts between groups, now that each group is taking charge of their own resource distribution algorithms/AI models'.

This problem can be solved by 'increasing interactivity between groups as much as possible', so that each individual is a member of multiple groups, to avoid excess inter-group conflict.

It can also be solved by each group committing to a 'group constitution' where each group commits to sharing AI/algorithm inventions between all groups.

This 'group constitution' would handle cases where a 'group exploits another group' and other 'problems of group dynamics' to 'protect the rights of each group, and also protect a relevant majority/group of groups'.

'Solving inter-group conflict' is related to 'solving inter-group inequality', which means all groups should be similar in ways like 'all groups should include some ratio of neuro-diverse, intelligent individuals'.

Groups can also agree to an 'algorithm/constitution' to resolve inter-group conflicts.


## Solving group dependence

Each individual should be independent of their groups, in case they want to change their groups or build new groups.

Learning how to build/invent AI is a way to solve this problem.

Each individual can also be meaningful/relevant without a group in this way.

In this way, a case where 'only groups (or only large groups) have rights' can be avoided.
