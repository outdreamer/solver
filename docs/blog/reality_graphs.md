# Reality graphs

Generating the right graph can help with intents like 'solving reality'.


## Workflows to generate graphs

Example workflow: 
1. generate possible reality types/variants (for example, a 'reality that controls other realities' or a 'reality that fulfills a rate of variation' or a 'reality that cycles with other related realities')
2. identify possible errors in the 'reality types/variants' and indicators of these errors
3. group the types/variants into 'coordinating sets' (like 'sets of reality types/variants that can co-exist')
4. extend each reality type/variant set with implications/relevance of what that reality type/variant could mean if it interacted with a known structure (like an entropy limit or a universal constant), until the implications connect to known structures like 'sets of coordinating facts'
5. identify possible errors of the 'implications of reality types/variants' and indicators of these errors
6. filter the reality types/variants by connecting them to filters/tests (like connecting the implications of a reality type to a 'fact supporting that implication' or a 'test that can contradict it')
7. identify an optimized 'network of filters/tests' differentiating 'unique attributes of reality types/variants'
8. identify possible errors of the 'filters/tests in the network of filters/tests' and indicators of these errors
9. identify an optimized network of 'indicators of errors' of these structures (for example, to identify 'overlapping indicators of multiple error types')

This graph of possible reality structures, sets of coordinating reality structures, implications, facts connected to implications, filters/tests of the reality structures, errors of these structures, and optimized networks of these interactive structures like 'networks of errors' and 'networks of filters' is a useful starting point to generate other graphs.


## Workflows to generate maximally different reality structures

- 'realities that can exist, given the same set of facts/constants' like 'maximally different realities allowed by a set of definitions' are 'variants of realities that can exist' (which is useful to identify 'adjacent realities' and 'causal networks of realities', where identifying 'realities generated by multiple/relevant different sets of constants' is useful to generate an 'optimized network of realities'), which can be generated with a workflow like 'apply variation to constants'
- applying workflows/metrics like 'mixed differentiation/uncertainty/interface variables' (given that reality often reflects a mix of interface variables like 'expected and counterintuitive structures') can be useful to generate 'reality structure connections' like 'connecting counterintuitive reality structures and expected reality structures'


## Other useful graphs to generate

- graphs with 'coordinating fact sets' as nodes to connect with reality structures (connecting 'sets of facts' with the 'reality structures they imply/confirm/generate')
- graphs of 'maximally different reality structures' which are the most difficult to connect with the same 'coordinating fact sets'
- graphs of 'reality structures' and 'related perspectives' like 'causal perspectives of a reality structure' as a variant of a 'set of facts'
- graphs of 'reality variables' to generate 'maximally different reality structures'
- graphs of 'reality limits' to generate 'possibility spaces of reality structures'
- graphs of 'implications of other graphs' and 'optimized connections between implications of other graphs'
