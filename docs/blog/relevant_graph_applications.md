# Applications of relevant graphs

Relevant graphs can be applied in many ways, like as components of other graphs, or components of algorithms to compare connections across graphs.


## Examples

Examples of applications of relevant graphs:
- applying relevant graphs (like graphs of complexity and graphs of relevance) for comparisons to identify errors/solutions, like to identify when an increase in complexity is an error, and when an increase in complexity is not an error based on whether an increase in complexity corresponds to a relatively higher increase in relevance
- applying relevant graphs as structures on other graphs, like applying a concentric system layer of one graph as a filter of a boundary space of another graph, to integrate relevant graphs into more relevant graphs
- applying relevant graphs to identify cross-graph metrics, like cross-graph optimality/other relevance
- applying relevant graphs for 'identifying meaning/relevance', to regularly check the meaning of generated/identified structures
- applying relevant graphs as a 'base of truth to apply changes to' or as a 'limit of possible changes', in order to identify/generate new truths like 'new relevant graphs'
- applying relevant graphs as an explicit/defined/certain/direct relevant force, as an opposing force to implicit/probable/uncertain/indirect relevance, to increase the certainty/other relevance of a function/structure
- applying relevant graphs to represent different similarity/difference structures and the structures connectible with those similarity/difference structures
- applying relevant graphs to make all relevant connections 'trivial/otherwise relevant to connect' on a relevant graph to solve problems on relevant graphs
- applying relevant graphs as components of sequences of graphs, like for graph intent sequences like 'intent sequences of generating/organizing/integrating/optimizing/reducing relevant graphs'
- applying relevant graphs to define relevance/meaning
- applying relevant graphs to implement generally relevant problem-solving intents like 'optimize systems'
- applying relevant graphs to connect all relevant graphs in a graph of relevant graphs
- applying relevant graphs to optimize queries for all relevant structures of optimization metrics, so that all queries are optimized on 'at least one relevant graph or multiple relevant graphs' or other optimization structure of relevant graphs for all relevant structures of optimization metrics like 'relevant combinations of optimization metrics'
- applying relevant graphs to implement intents like 'identify whether a new concept is required, by whether a problem is trivially/relevantly solvable on a relevant graph'
- applying relevant graphs to identify optimal graph variables by comparing more/less relevant graphs
- applying relevant graphs to implement perspectives (with associated errors, priorities, definitions of similarity, structures to connect, intents to implement, etc)
- applying relevant graphs like 'spectrums' to identify 'similarities/patterns in relevant graphs'
