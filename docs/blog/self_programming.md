# Self-programming

It's useful to identify different requirements/intents/possibilities of self-programming, or hypnosis.

Useful requirements of hypnosis:
- 'authoritative repetition of simple/general/relevant intents (right before sleep, since sleep is when the brain has the maximum available resources)' is important
- 'using language the brain understands' is also important ('repair the area that hurts' is likelier to be understood by your brain than 'repair this specific cell type')
- 'solving the problem for the brain (or 'doing the thinking', as in 'pre-computing info')' by 'identifying the specific intents/structures that the brain should change' is similarly important, when specific intents are understood by the brain

It's also useful to identify intents that the brain might not already be optimized for:
- structure-organization intents like 'identifying the most useful way to use energy' (like by 'attacking the biggest/worst problems first', 'applying parallel sequences of intents, one step at a time', etc) is an intent that might not be already optimized in the brain, meaning your brain doesnt do it by default and instead likely applies another algorithm to allocate resources (like 'prioritize fixing organ damage first'), but by thinking of optimal sequences/sets/structures of intents, you can do some of the thinking for your brain before giving it instructions
- generally useful intents like 'increasing energy' and 'creating new neurons' and 'increase resilience' and 'increase strength' and 'clearing dead structures and repairing damaged structures' and 'increasing immunity' and 'increasing recovery/repair' and 'decreasing inflammation' can also help enable other intents in general
- feedback-based intents like 'identify substances/habits that are useful for other intents and create cravings for those substances/habits' and 'identify harmful substances/habits and create aversions to those substances/habits' is useful to enable other intents
- meta-intents like 'apply the other intents in a sequence optimized for maximum success rates across the most intents' is similarly unlikely to already be a default brain configuration/pattern

What is it possible/impossible to change, using self-programming?
- brain-microbiome interactions, brain responses to sensory experiences (like pain), brain generation of chemicals such as neurotransmitters, inter-neuron communication/connectivity/activation, self-interaction brain processes (like brain cleaning), and other brain interactions are possible to change
- 'neuron count' is an especially important variable to change (by increasing neuron count, more problems can be solved, more possibilities can be explored, more stress can be handled, etc), similar to how 'neuron connectivity/specialization' is important
- the limit of how much energy can be produced, what intents that energy can be used for, how organs naturally work, and other physical limits are unlikely to change with self-programming
