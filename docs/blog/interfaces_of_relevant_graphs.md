# Interfaces of relevant graphs

Interface structures like 'limits/errors/integrations' of relevant graphs are useful to identify.


## Types of relevant graphs

Relevant graphs have types like:
- graphs applying an interface structure like a similarity as a core component
- graphs connecting cross-interface structures like connecting all interface variables
- graphs of similarities crossing the implementation spectrum (from identifying generative variables of a graph, to implementing it, to optimizing it, to reducing it)
- graphs that optimize/integrate/organize/connect other graphs
- graphs with relevant embedded spectrums like 'abstraction'
- graphs with relevant emergent similarities


## Limits of relevant graphs

A relevant graph is limited by:
- 'available computation/identification/evaluation potential' like by 'what other relevant graphs can identify/evaluate/generate regarding the relevant graph'
- a relevant graph is limited in the sense of being static/constant when queries are being run on it, but otherwise it can be optimized by updating it to implement system optimization rules like 'update a system with new information'


## Integrations of relevant graphs

Integrations of relevant graphs include:
- graphs of relevant graphs
- graphs generated by integrations of relevant components like 'concentric layers of graphs' or 'relevant axes across graphs'
- graphs of combinations/integrations/structures of relevant graphs


## Errors of relevant graphs

Relevant graphs have errors like:
- optimizing for only a subset of optimization metrics, like applying 'multiple' interface structures
- being relevant only because the problem solved by the graph hasnt been invalidated by other graphs yet
- differing in relevance over their implementation lifecycle, and in relation to other relevant graphs


## Optimizations of relevant graphs

Relevance graphs have optimizations like:
- increasing the relevance of a graph by optimizing it for additional relevance definition components without invalidating other optimizations
- increasing the relevance of other graphs by optimizing it for evaluating/generating/optimizing other relevant graphs
