# Integrations of relevance and neural networks

Integrating relevance in neural networks increases their predictive ability, similar to how training neural networks on 'interface data' like 'interface definitions' increases their predictive ability because interface structures are highly predictive, and similar to how integrating relevant graphs with neural networks increases their predictive ability by applying 'identified connections of interface variables' from the relevant graphs. 


## Examples of integrations of relevance and neural networks

### Component examples

Cross-relevance structure examples:
- cross-relevance structures like intents/functions or functions/structures as component vectors of relevance multiplied to generate relevance

Relevant interface interaction examples:
- applying interface variables as different 'parameters of polynomial functions' with varying coefficients indicating the different interactions of interface variables


### Vector sequence examples

Relevance implementation examples:
- relevant (like maximally different) vector sequences, as relevance implementation structures

Relevance generation examples:
- different relevance structures of polynomials like 'different ways to generate polynomials' (like different component types like subsets of polynomials and parameters of polynomials and polynomial types like waves) as different sequences in the network


### Network/variable subset connection examples

Relevant problem-solving examples:
- different subsets of the network applied to identify polynomial functions connecting different relevant variable sets like 'maximally different variable sets' as 'problems to solve in the network', where different subsets generating polynomials are organized in the network by what polynomials are useful to generate other polynomials
